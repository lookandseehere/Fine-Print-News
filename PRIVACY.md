# Privacy Policy — Fine Print News

*Last updated: November 7, 2026. Draft: have it reviewed before public launch, and update it whenever the app changes how it handles data.*

## The short version
- No accounts, no ads and no tracking.
- Fine Print News doesn't collect your name, email, location or anything you type.
- Everything the app remembers stays in your own browser.

## What stays on your device
The app saves these things in your own browser, using its "local storage":
- your answers in "What's missing?" and any question you write
- which story sections and cards you opened

This data never leaves your device. We can't see it.

You can delete it at any time in either of these ways:
- clear your browser's site data
- uninstall the app

## What the app loads from elsewhere
- **Fonts** come from Google Fonts. Your browser contacts Google's servers to download them, and Google receives the standard technical information any website visit sends, such as your IP address. See Google's privacy policy.
- **Links** to official sources open those websites. Their own privacy policies apply.

## Hosting
The app is hosted by Netlify. Like any web host, Netlify may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and request times, for security and operations. Fine Print News doesn't use these logs to identify readers.

## If this changes
We will update this policy before adding anything that collects or shares information, such as comparing your picks with other readers, analytics, notifications or accounts. If anything is ever collected, it will follow these laws:
- BC's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA)
- Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA)
