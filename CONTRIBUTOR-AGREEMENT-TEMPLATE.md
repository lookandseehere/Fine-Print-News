This agreement is between **[Your full legal name]**, operating as Fine Print News ("Fine Print News"), and **[Contributor name]** ("Contributor").

4. **Confidentiality.** Contributor will keep confidential any non-public plans, code, content, data and business information about Fine Print News. This continues after this agreement ends.

5. **Original work.** Contributor confirms the Work is their own and does not copy anyone else's protected material without permission.

6. **Payment.** [Amount and schedule, or "Volunteer; no payment"].

7. **Independence.**
   - Contributor is an independent contractor or volunteer, not an employee.
   - Contributor will not speak for Fine Print News publicly without permission.
