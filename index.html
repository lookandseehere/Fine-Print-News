    Why I built this

    Built by a fed-up news reader.

    Honestly, I got so frustrated with the news. Everything felt twisted or divided, like I was being told what to think and which side I was on before I even knew what had actually happened. And I could see it in the people around me too, no matter how they vote.

    So I finally said, okay, it's time to find another way. I started going straight to the source: the actual release, the actual numbers. Turns out it's all there. It's just buried in the fine print.

    That's what this is. The facts straight from the source, in plain language, plus a few questions to help you think it through for yourself. No spin, and no one telling you what to believe. The whole point is to get you thinking critically, not to do the thinking for you. Just the fine print, and then you decide.

    — Kyra, Squamish, BC

    About, privacy & policies
    Mission
    To help people think critically about what their government says and does: the original source first, then good questions instead of ready-made answers. We never tell you what to think.
    Independent
    Fine Print News is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or speaking for the Government of Canada, Elections Canada, the Government of British Columbia or Elections BC.
    Sources
    Facts are restated in our own words from official public sources, and every story links to the original. The original is the authoritative version. If anything here differs from it, the source wins.
    Privacy
    No accounts and no ads. Your answers stay on your device. To improve the app, we count anonymously how far people get in “What’s missing?” (which cards they reach), never names and never anything you type. If you choose “Add my picks,” only the options you tapped are shared with other readers, as totals.
    Corrections
    Corrections will be listed publicly. Contact details for reporting an error are coming at launch.
